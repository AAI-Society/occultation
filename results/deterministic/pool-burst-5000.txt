On exhaustion there are two available behaviours. This tool stalls. It does not
reuse, and reuse is not configurable: two presentations sharing one blinding
factor are linkable on sight, and an observer of both transcripts recovers the
signature scalar and every undisclosed attribute by subtraction.

Stalling is not free either. The amplification figure above is the size of the
timing side channel: how far a busy agent's response time departs from its idle
one, and therefore how much of its load an observer can read off latency alone.
It is the peak over the observation window and grows linearly with --duration, so
it characterises the window as much as the pool; the overload factor and growth
per request above are its duration-invariant companions and are the figures to
quote as a property of this pool rather than of this run.

The stall does not plateau. While arrivals outrun refill the backlog grows, so
each successive request waits longer than the last and the delay tracks the
burst's duration — which is what makes it readable rather than merely costly.
